Voice

“Hey Siri, Kosi”

Make a Shortcut once, and Siri opens Kosi listening, from anywhere, even the lock screen.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +.
  2. Add the action Open URLs, and paste this link:
  3. Tap the name at the top and call it Kosi. Done: say “Hey Siri, Kosi”.

iPhone opens Shortcut links in Safari, not in the Home Screen app, and the two keep separate settings. So set Kosika up in Safari too: tap Copy my setup here, open the copied link in Safari once, and your key and palette are there as well.

A quick answer without opening anything

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (15 Pro or newer), a second Shortcut answers out loud with Apple's model on the phone, even offline:

  1. New Shortcut. Add Dictate Text.
  2. Add Use Model, choose On-Device, and write: You are Kosi, Aryan's friendly helper. Answer in two or three short spoken sentences: then insert Dictated Text.
  3. Add Speak Text with the Response. Name it Ask Kosi.
