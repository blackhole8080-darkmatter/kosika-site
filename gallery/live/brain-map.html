The seven regions

Memory grows

Today: episode, fact, recipe, structure, recalled by meaning or words above a floor. Adds working memory (this conversation) and an episodic timeline of everything Kosika did, with a short daily summary written each night.

Knowledge graph new

Facts as links: Kosika —written in→ Go. Each link keeps its source, date and a confidence that comes from sources and checks, never from a model rating itself. Two facts that disagree are flagged, not merged.

Thinking, two speeds new

Fast: code rules and the small local model (qwen3 1.7B on CPU). Slow: a bigger free model, only when fast is unsure. On anything that matters, two models must agree or a third breaks the tie.

Checking has

Kosika's own checkers do the exact work: maths computed by std/math, code proven by tests, quotes matched word for word. An answer that fails a check never reaches you as true.

Learning grows

Today: kept canvases, ratings and a small network that learns which drawing fits. Next: learns from every checked run and every Keep or Drop, weekly. A new model or rule replaces the old one only when the scorecard goes up.

You new

What the Brain knows about you: style (Clear in Studio, bold art elsewhere), lines you hold (no paying, yes before writes), what you study. Shown to you, editable by you, never shared.

Curiosity grows

Today: gathers arXiv papers as facts. Next: in idle time it finds its weak spots (shaky facts, failed asks), reads, and proposes fixes on your phone. Nothing changes until you say yes.

Mind view new

A live Designs canvas of this picture: regions light up as the Brain recalls, thinks and checks. The orb glows in the same colours while Kosi speaks.

One loop for every ask

sense→recall→think→check→act→learn

Same loop as today. The change is that it stops living inside Designs and becomes brain_think(ask, part), so Kosika AI, the swarm, Kosi and Chat share one memory and get smarter together.

Build order, one step at a time

1

One core and the timeline

brain_think in std/brain.kos, working memory and the episodic timeline. Kosika AI is the first part wired through it. Tests in both engines, Go first.

2

Knowledge graph with sources

Facts as links with source, date and checked confidence. arXiv and Wikipedia reads land here. Meaning recall with a small local embedding model (nomic-embed-text, about 270 MB, fine on CPU).

3

Two speeds and agreement

Fast path first, slow free model when unsure, two must agree. Measured on the Kosika AI scorecard (10/20 today).

4

Nightly summary and weekly learning

Episodes become facts and recipes overnight. Weekly, Keep/Drop and checked runs retrain; promotion only when the score goes up.

5

You, and curiosity

The user model you can read and edit, and idle-time proposals sent to your phone for a yes.

Mind view
